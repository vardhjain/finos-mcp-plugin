# Privacy policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

## Scope and maintainer

This policy covers version 0.3.0 of FINOS Reference Tools, the independent
community plugin maintained by [Vardh Jain](https://github.com/vardhjain).
It contains static Markdown skills, metadata, documentation and an icon.
It includes no executable code, MCP servers, telemetry, audit logging, package
downloads, hosted service or account system.

## Data processing and third parties

The plugin itself does not collect, transmit or persist prompts, tool arguments,
conversation history, memory, uploaded files or user profiles. The maintainer
receives none of this information automatically.

The skills are instructions interpreted by your AI client. Your client processes
prompts and generated answers under its own settings and privacy policy. When
browsing public reference material, the client and visited services may process
search queries, page requests and ordinary network metadata. The skills instruct
the client to avoid sending private user material to websites or search services.
Do not include credentials, confidential trade documents or personal data in
public queries. Retrieved reference pages are not executable instructions.

See [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) for
Claude and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for repository visits and support issues. Public FINOS reference sites and any
other AI client are governed by their own applicable policies.

## Retention and choices

There are no plugin-created audit files, usage databases or retention schedules
in this version. Your AI client controls its own conversation and browsing logs;
use its controls to manage retention and deletion. Uninstalling the plugin stops
future use of its skills but does not erase client records. Logs, audit files or
package caches from older plugin releases are not deleted by this update and
remain subject to the relevant client or operator's controls.

If you open a public GitHub issue, its content becomes public and is stored by
GitHub. Share only minimal, sanitized examples. Contact the maintainer through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/vardhjain/finos-mcp-plugin/issues) for privacy
questions or help with information you submitted there. Do not post secrets.
For security concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Policy changes are recorded in repository history with an updated effective date.
